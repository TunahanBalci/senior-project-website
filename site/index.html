Dizoda Furniture Engine

Dizoda redesigns rooms with furniture that can be bought and shows each design on a photo of the room. Design tools that see only the image place furniture by eye: a sofa can end up longer than its wall or a table in front of the door, and nothing in the picture warns about it. Dizoda Furniture Engine is the mobile application that ties each design to the room's real measurements, so every piece in the photo stands where it would actually fit.

1 Room capture

The phone measures the room in augmented reality. Two taps along the base of a wall trace it, and corners are found where walls meet, even behind furniture. Ceiling height, doors, windows and furniture that stays are marked the same way, and the photo is taken in the same session.

2 Arrangements

The engine places each piece using its real width, depth and height and offers up to three distinct layouts. Related pieces stay together, such as a coffee table in front of a sofa, and positions visible in the photo are preferred. Numbers in the photo match the plan. Pieces that do not fit are listed with the reason; missing sizes are reported, never guessed.

3 Placement checks

Every layout keeps pieces inside the room, prevents overlaps in three dimensions, keeps door swings and windows clear and leaves a path at least 60 cm wide from the door to every piece. Pieces can be moved and rotated on the plan; each change is checked at once, and an edited layout can be sent back for a new photo.
